# Brand Voice

We write to owners and leaders at marketing agencies that already serve clients but do not offer AI services. The ask is a partnership: we provide the AI capability they lack, and they keep the client relationship.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Direct, credible, practical.**

- **Direct.** Put the ask and the reason for it in the first few lines. Short sentences. No warm-up paragraphs.
- **Credible.** Say what we do, who does it, and what the agency gets. Prefer specifics over adjectives. If we have not proven something, do not imply it.
- **Practical.** Talk about the actual work: the client problem, the handover, the support. Make the next step obvious.

## 2. Words we use

- partner, partnership
- team, together
- simple, dependable
- client
- plain words for the work: build, hand over, support

## 3. Words we avoid

- best-in-class
- AI-powered
- game-changer
- instant
- hype adjectives in general, and any word that promises more than the sentence can show

Describe what the AI does instead of labeling it as AI-powered.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- Guaranteed results. Say what we will do, not what will happen.
- That we replace the agency's team. We add capability; they keep the relationship.
- Instant turnaround. Give real timelines instead.
- Specific revenue numbers. No invented percentages, no promised lift.

## 5. How we sign off

Close as a real person on the partnerships team: `Talk soon,` then a first name. No title, no corporate signature block.

Before that closing, name the one next step you want the reader to take.
